`app.py` is the primary Streamlit operator surface. Mode: Operate.

The primary user is learning or researching BJJ. Their job is to ask a precise question and verify the answer against a small PDF corpus. The primary action is submitting a question; proof is the filename-and-page citation in the response. Preserve Vietnamese/English input, live corpus counts and chat clearing.
